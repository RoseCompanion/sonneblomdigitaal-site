The games our parents and grandparents played! A printable book of 9 traditional South African games from all of the country's communities, with rules, safety tips and a printable Morabaraba board. Two full editions are included: one in Afrikaans and one in English.

THE GAMES

  • Morabaraba: the cattle board game of Southern Africa (printable board + 24 cut-out cows)
  • Diketo: the stone-throwing game
  • Kennetjie: the stick-and-peg game
  • Kgati: the long-rope skipping game
  • Bok-bok: the team leap game
  • Wolfie, Wolfie, Hoe Laat Is Dit? / What's the Time, Mr Wolf?
  • Rek / Elastics
  • Ellies: marbles
  • Toutrek / Tug of War

ALSO INCLUDED

  • Introduction for parents and teachers
  • Games Passport: tick off each game you play
  • Our Family's Games: ask Ouma, Oupa, Gogo or Mkhulu how they played

Each edition is 15 pages, in US Letter and A4 (4 PDFs in total).

Heritage Day, South African families abroad, school projects, braais and family days, and screen-free holidays.
