Keep Afrikaans alive at home! "Kom Ontdek Suid-Afrika" is a 20-page activity book for kids aged 4 to 10, written fully in Afrikaans and filled with South African animals, places and food.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (20 pages, US Letter + A4):

  • Alles Oor My (all about me) fill-in page
  • My Afrikaanse Woordeboek: a page to collect new Afrikaans words
  • 3 word searches (Woordsoek): easy, medium and hard, with SA animals and places
  • Deurmekaar Woorde: 15 scrambled South African words (braai, biltong, melktert...)
  • 3 mazes (Doolhowe): easy, medium and hard
  • Ek Sien Met My Klein Ogie: I Spy counting page
  • Sou Jy Eerder...? (Would you rather), 18 questions
  • Teken en Skryf: draw and write
  • Lekker Dinge om te Doen: 18-item bucket list
  • Kruisies en Nulletjies (tic-tac-toe)
  • 12 Afrikaans riddle cards (Raaisels) to cut out
  • Afrikaanse Ster certificate
  • Answer keys

PERFECT FOR
Afrikaans families abroad, Afrikaans home-schoolers, Sunday school, holidays with Ouma and Oupa, and long flights home.
