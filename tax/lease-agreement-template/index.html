What a South African lease must include

The Rental Housing Act requires a written lease that states the names and addresses of the parties, a description of the dwelling, the rent and any escalation, the deposit, the lease period and notice period, the tenant's and landlord's obligations, and that the incoming and outgoing inspections will be done together. This template covers all of these.

Consumer Protection Act

If you let property as a business (even one property is often enough) and the tenant is a person, the CPA applies: the tenant can cancel a fixed-term lease on 20 business days' notice (with a reasonable penalty), you must remind the tenant 40 to 80 business days before the term ends, and you must give 20 business days to fix a breach before cancelling.

This template is a starting point, not legal advice. For unusual situations (commercial property, sub-letting, a business run from home) have an attorney review it.
