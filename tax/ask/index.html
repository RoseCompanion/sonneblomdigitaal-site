TaxBot: ask a South African tax question

Plain-English answers about SARS, PAYE, VAT, deductions, provisional tax and CGT, with the section of the Act and the SARS guide it comes from. 5 free questions a day.

AI trained on SA tax rulesCites the Act5 free questions a day
Hi! I'm TaxBot. Ask me anything about South African tax, for example: • "Can I claim my home office if I work from home 3 days a week?" • "Do I pay tax on money my parents give me?" • "How is a retrenchment package taxed?"

TaxBot gives general information, not advice for your exact situation. Don't type ID numbers, tax numbers or bank details.

What can TaxBot help with?

  • Whether you need to file, register for provisional tax or register for VAT
  • What you can deduct: home office, travel, RA, medical, donations (s18A)
  • How bonuses, retrenchment packages, lump sums, crypto, rental and side income are taxed
  • SARS letters, verification, penalties, objections and payment arrangements
  • Employer questions: PAYE, UIF, SDL, EMP201/EMP501, fringe benefits

For numbers, use the income tax calculator. For the actual return, eFiling or a registered tax practitioner is still the final word.

Frequently asked questions

Is TaxBot free?

Yes, 5 questions a day are free. Pro gives you unlimited questions.

Is TaxBot a registered tax practitioner?

No. It's an AI assistant that explains SA tax rules and points to the law and SARS guides. For advice on your specific return, use a registered tax practitioner.
