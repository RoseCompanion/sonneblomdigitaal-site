Rent tracker

See who paid, who's late and how much is outstanding. Free for up to 2 tenants and saved in this browser. With Pro, upload your bank statement and FinBot marks the rent payments for you.

Free for 2 tenantsPro: reads your bank statementSaved in your browser

🏦 Mark payments from a bank statement PRO

How the rent tracker works

  • Add each tenant with the monthly rent and the reference or name they use when paying.
  • Type amounts as rent comes in, or (Pro) drop your bank statement and FinBot finds each tenant's payment by reference and amount.
  • Green = paid in full, yellow = part paid, red = nothing received. The Owing column totals the last 6 months.

Your list is saved in this browser only. The statement is read and deleted immediately; only the matched payments come back.

Behind on rent? Send a letter of demand. At tax time, use the rental income tax calculator.
